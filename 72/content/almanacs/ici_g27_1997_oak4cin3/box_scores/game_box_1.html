Bench Flies Out With Bases Loaded as A's Survive 7th-Inning Rally
Fifty-three degrees at game time and something cold blowing across the Coliseum at eleven miles an hour. With two out in the first and Matty Alou on first, Sal Bando took a called strike, fouled off two pitches, took three balls and fouled off a full-count pitch before he hit the eighth one 395 feet to left. Two runs, and Don Gullett was already behind.

Alou added a solo shot in the third, fighting out of an 0-2 hole by fouling off two more before he put one 390 feet over the right-field fence. Dave Concepcion tripled and scored on Gullett's infield single in the fifth, and Angel Mangual answered in the bottom half with a single to right that scored Alou. Oakland led 4-1, and Ken Holtzman, who would strike out eight, had the Reds hitting into double plays: Menke in the sixth, McRae in the seventh.

McRae's came right after Pete Rose reached on Holtzman's own error, and it left two out and nobody on. Then Concepcion singled. Pinch-hitter Cesar Geronimo singled. Joe Morgan singled home Concepcion, and Bobby Tolan singled home Geronimo. Four straight two-out hits, and Holtzman was gone. Rollie Fingers walked Tony Perez on a full count to load the bases with Oakland up a run, and Johnny Bench came to the plate. Fingers threw him one pitch. Bench flew out to left.

McRae singled off Fingers in the eighth and went nowhere. Vida Blue needed five pitches for the ninth: pinch-hitter Joe Hague flew to center, Morgan flew to right, and Tolan bounced back to the mound. Oakland led the Series three games to one.
