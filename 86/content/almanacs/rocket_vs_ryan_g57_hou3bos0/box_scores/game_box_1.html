Astros Crowned Champions
Kevin Bass, Houston's right fielder, came up in the bottom of the second with one out and nobody on, and Roger Clemens, in the middle of a tidy night that would carry him through eight innings on 96 pitches, threw him one he shouldn't have. Bass drove it out through the heavy still air of the dome, air that killed fly balls for a living and let this one go anyway, and the three-story scoreboard ran the bull through its whole act, pistols and spaceships and cattle brands. It was Bass's sixth home run of this series. All six had come off Clemens. Two of them, this one and one from some other Sunday, had come in the second inning with one out and the bases empty, like a man who had parked in the same spot at the grocery store twice by accident.

An inning later Bill Doran was aboard with one out when Billy Hatcher, the Houston center fielder, hit a two-run homer, the bull came back out for an encore, and at 3-0 that was the ballgame. Clemens allowed three runs on five hits, all of the runs on those two swings. He went 0-for-3 at the plate with two strikeouts before Don Baylor hit for him in the ninth. In the seventh he gave up a two-out double to Nolan Ryan, who stood on second in his rainbow guts like a Texas sunset somebody had left on the bag, and was stranded there.

Ryan went eight and a third innings, 133 pitches, ten strikeouts, four walks, five hits, no runs. Boston kept putting men on and leaving them. Marty Barrett went 2-for-3 and led off the sixth with a double that went nowhere. Dwight Evans struck out three times. Ryan got one out in the ninth, a man reached, and Charlie Kerfeld came in and got the last two for the save.

Houston, a franchise that had never been to a World Series, had won a game in one with two swings and one old man's arm, and 46,101 people under the roof made a noise like a gymnasium full of car horns. Ryan sat on the top dugout step with his elbows on his knees, watching Kerfeld finish a game he had pitched nearly all of, and did not move until the ball was dead.
