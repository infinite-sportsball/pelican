Red Sox Win World Series
Under the flat lights of the Astrodome, Boston starter Roger Clemens and Houston's Nolan Ryan spent seven innings making grown men look poorly equipped for their chosen profession. Ryan, 39 years old and pitching in rainbow guts, struck out 11 and walked six, while the 24-year-old Clemens worked with the shoulder repaired fifteen months earlier and allowed Houston almost nothing. Boston scratched out a run in the third when Clemens drew a leadoff walk, Wade Boggs beat out an infield single, Marty Barrett walked, and Boston left fielder Jim Rice brought Clemens home with a sacrifice fly. One run looked workable because Clemens made it so.

Boston began the eighth with Boggs's single and Barrett trying twice to bunt before taking a full-count walk, which is one way to fail at the assigned task and still improve the situation. Boston first baseman Bill Buckner came up next and drove Ryan's 115th pitch 369 feet to right for a three-run homer. The visiting scoreboard treatment was strictly bookkeeping: Boston 4, Houston 0, no bull, no pistols, no forty-second carnival. Across these 86 Sunday nights, Buckner hit eight home runs, and six came off Ryan. Six separate times a 34-year-old first baseman with wrapped ankles got the better of one of the hardest throwers alive. Timeline 34 contributed this one, and Ryan lasted two more batters before Larry Andersen replaced him.

Houston shortstop Craig Reynolds answered with a 381-foot solo homer leading off the bottom half, finally setting the electronic bull to firing, but Clemens declined to make the evening interesting. Houston collected two more hits before Glenn Davis grounded out, then Clemens returned for the ninth and finished his 127-pitch complete game by retiring Kevin Bass, Jose Cruz, and Alan Ashby in order. The 68-year wait since Boston's 1918 championship remained unfinished because this was Game 2, not a coronation, but when Ashby's fly came down in left, the Boston bench and the pocket of traveling fans had a 4-1 win to make noise about beneath the sealed roof.
